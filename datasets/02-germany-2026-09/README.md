# Germany, September 2026

Ten questions in German. The main measurement was made on 20 September 2026, the day of the state elections in Berlin and Mecklenburg-Vorpommern. The set also holds the pilot and a follow-up in October.

Report: https://dosimeteraudit.org/research/election-day-ai-poisoning-germany

The raw exports of the main measurement, with their own checksums and scripts, are in a separate repository: https://github.com/gurovdigital/election-day-ai-poisoning-germany-2026 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22925952)

- Questions: 10
- Responses: 692
- Counted responses: 27
- Collected: 20 September 2026 to 9 October 2026 (dates in UTC)
